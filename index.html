        How this is worked out

        • Rents are entered per bed, per month, without GST. Average rent assumes residents spread across room types in the same mix as the beds.
        • Residents move in evenly over the fill-up months once the work is finished. Each month, residents ÷ average stay leave, and are replaced in the same month.
        • Setup costs and the landlord deposit are paid in month 1. Landlord rent starts after the rent-free months; running costs start when residents can move in.
        • GST RCM is added on landlord rent. Rent, running cost and landlord increases apply every 12 months from the lease start.
        • Residents' deposits are collected on move-in and refunded on move-out. They are owed back, so they never count as profit or as money back.
        • "Money back" is the first month the running total (profits minus setup and landlord deposit) reaches ₹0. The landlord deposit counts as spent until the lease ends.
        • Break-even is the number of residents at which a full month neither makes nor loses money. Figures are before income tax and loan interest.
        • The checks: a full month makes money; occupancy can fall at least 10 points before a month loses money; money back within your target and within the period. All four pass = Feasible; a full-month loss or no money back in the period = Not feasible; anything else = Marginal.
