Gaps in the source workbook

    How the figures are built

    • Monthly figures are the workbook's own monthly Total columns on the Cash Flow sheet; weekly figures are its week columns. Nothing is estimated.
    • Closing balance = opening + inflows − outflows + Razorpay balance movement (row 91). Gaps where a week opens away from the prior close show as "carry-forward adjustments".
    • Jan–Mar 2024 exist only as monthly totals. Nov-26 weeks 3–5 (13–30 Nov) are typed-in projections.
    • Operating / investing / financing is a proposed mapping, not a tag in the file. Investing: . Financing: . Everything else is operating. The Kakatiya Project reimbursement inflow is paired with the Kakatiya Project outflow as investing. The file's "Renovation Expense" heading is a subtotal of maintenance, housekeeping and supplies, so it is classed operating.
    • Months of runway needs a positive cash balance. While the account is overdrawn it shows n/a; no overdraft limit is recorded in the file.
    • Transactions come from the Bank Statements FY 24-26 and Razorpay statements sheets. Their totals can differ from the cash-flow sheet, which carries manual adjustments; each drill-down shows both.
    • No budget, customer-wise receipts or forecast beyond the file's own Nov-26 projections exist in the workbook, so none are shown.
